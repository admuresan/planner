QUICK ADD FEATURE - COMPLETE

✅ FEATURE IMPLEMENTED

Hover over empty calendar space to see a plus icon.
Click it to create an event at that exact time!

==========================================
HOW TO USE
==========================================

1. Hover over any empty space in the calendar
2. Wait 300ms - a green plus icon appears
3. Click the plus icon
4. Modal opens with date/time pre-filled
5. Just type event name and save!
