INFINITE SCROLL - IMPLEMENTATION COMPLETE
==========================================

✅ FEATURE IMPLEMENTED

As you scroll horizontally, the calendar now:
1. Automatically loads more days
2. Loads events for those days
3. Always keeps 2+ weeks ahead of what you're viewing
4. Works seamlessly in both directions

==========================================
HOW IT WORKS
==========================================

Scroll Right (Future):
- Get within 7 days of the right edge
- System adds 14 days automatically
- Events load for those new dates
- No interruption to scrolling

Scroll Left (Past):
- Get within 7 days of the left edge
- System adds 14 days to the beginning
- Events load for historical dates
- Scroll position stays smooth
